  4501→precisely when $\delta$ has exactly two nonzero coordinates of equal magnitude
  4502→and opposite sign; direct maximization over $k=2,\dots,12$ returns
  4503→$1.000000000000$.  For the composite, $\inf\{D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\|q)/
  4504→\|p-q\|_2^2\}$ over distinct $p,q\in\Delta^{k-1}$ equals $1$ and is not
  4505→attained: over $58{,}186$ deliberately near-degenerate pairs evaluated in
  4506→$60$-digit arithmetic the minimum observed ratio is $1.0000000053$.  Along the
  4507→extremal family $p=(\tfrac12+t,\tfrac12-t)$, $q=(\tfrac12-t,\tfrac12+t)$ one
  4508→has $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\|q)/\|p-q\|_2^2=1+\tfrac43t^2+O(t^4)$, decreasing to $1$.
  4509→
  4510→A caution on floating-point evaluation: at separations $\|p-q\|_2^2\sim10^{-12}$
  4511→the ratio computed in double precision can read below $1$ purely through
  4512→cancellation in the logarithms.  Re-evaluating the same pairs in extended
  4513→precision restores the inequality.
  4514→\end{remark}

  4516→\begin{remark}[Numerical Verification]
  4517→\label{rem:retention-numerical}
  4518→The predictive-information identity of Theorem~\ref{thm:predictive-info} is an
  4519→exact identity and can be checked numerically on finite instances: over
  4520→stationary chains with $|\Splus|\le5$ and $|\mathcal O|\le4$, enumerating
  4521→\emph{all} set partitions, the three quantities $\RetKL(\phi)$,
  4522→$I(S;Z\mid K_\phi)$ and $I(S;Z)-I(K_\phi;Z)$ agree to $7.6\times10^{-16}$.
  4523→
  4524→Theorem~\ref{thm:global-kl-simplex} admits an end-to-end check of the same
  4525→kind, and each step of its proof can be isolated.  Over $2{,}434$
  4526→(instance, partition) pairs with $|\Splus|\le5$, $|\mathcal O|\le4$, again
  4527→enumerating all set partitions, the maximum violation is
  4528→$7.5\times10^{-17}$ for the minorant $\RetKL(\phi)\ge\tr(W_\phi)$,
  4529→$1.1\times10^{-16}$ for the ANOVA decomposition
  4530→$\Sigma_p=W_\phi+B_\phi$, $1.7\times10^{-16}$ for the Ky Fan bound
  4531→$\tr(B_\phi)\le\sum_{i\le M-1}\lambda_i$, and $7.5\times10^{-17}$ for the
  4532→composite conclusion $\RetKL(\phi)\ge\sum_{i\ge M}\lambda_i(\Sigma_p)$.  The
  4533→smallest ratio of the two sides over those pairs is $1.0015$, so the bound is
  4534→not vacuous on generic instances.
  4535→
  4536→Theorem~\ref{thm:global-interior-fisher} requires more care to test, since
  4537→$m_K$ is the least eigenvalue of $\nabla^2A$ over a convex set containing the
  4538→state parameters \emph{and} the centroid parameters, not a coarser proxy such
  4539→as the least predictive probability.  Computing $m_K$ by minimizing
  4540→$\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2A)$ over the convex hull gives $0$ violations on
  4541→$1{,}664$ (instance, partition) pairs, with least ratio $1.0011$; substituting
  4542→a proxy for $m_K$ produces spurious violations and tests a different statement.
  4543→
  4544→The quadratic-surrogate spectral bound can be checked directly on finite
  4545→weighted point clouds.  The local full-KL Fisher expansion can be checked on
  4546→small-radius exponential-family instances by verifying the predicted $r^2$
  4547→leading term and an $O(r^3)$ remainder.  These computations illustrate the
  4548→theorems; they do not establish their hypotheses.
  4549→\end{remark}

  4551→\begin{proposition}[Relation between Full-KL and Quadratic Restricted Gaps]
  4552→\label{prop:retkl-le-retquad}
  4553→In the Gaussian quadratic restricted regime, for every $M\ge1$,
  4554→\[
  4555→\RetKL(M)\le \RetQuad(M).
  4556→\]
  4557→\end{proposition}
  4558→
  4559→\begin{proof}
  4560→Fix a lumpable quotient $\phi$.  In the full-KL problem, the block
  4561→representative is unrestricted, and the optimal representative is the mixture
  4562→centroid $\bar P_k$.  In the quadratic restricted problem, the representative
  4563→is constrained to the fixed-covariance Gaussian family
  4564→$Q_c=\mathcal N(c,\tfrac12 I)$.  A constrained minimization problem has value
  4565→no smaller than the corresponding unconstrained problem.  Hence, for each
  4566→$\phi$,
  4567→\[
  4568→\RetKL(\phi)\le \RetQuad(\phi).
  4569→\]
  4570→Minimizing over lumpable quotients gives $\RetKL(M)\le \RetQuad(M)$.
  4571→\end{proof}

  4574→\subsection{Unifilar Retention and the Controlled Information Bottleneck}
  4575→\label{subsec:unifilar-retention}
  4576→%----------------------------------------------------------------------
  4577→
  4578→The retention theory extends from the input-driven model to general unifilar
  4579→machines without new analytical mechanism: the information-theoretic identities
  4580→depend only on the sufficiency structure $K=\phi(S)$, and the spectral
  4581→converses apply fiberwise in the input.  What does change is the feasible set,
  4582→which is now the unifilar-lumpable quotients of
  4583→Definition~\ref{def:unifilar-lumpable}, and with it the zero-retention
  4584→threshold.
  4585→
  4586→\begin{definition}[Controlled Full-KL Retention Gap]
  4587→\label{def:controlled-full-kl}
  4588→Let the input process be i.i.d.\ with law $p$ and independent of the state
  4589→process.  For a unifilar-lumpable quotient $\phi:\Splus\to\mathcal K$ with
  4590→blocks $C_k$ and representatives $\{Q_k^x\}$, set
  4591→\[
  4592→\RetKLc(\phi)
  4593→=
  4594→\sum_xp(x)\sum_k\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s\,
  4595→D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(P_s^x\,\big\|\,Q_k^x\bigr),
  4596→\]
  4597→the representatives being optimized for each $\phi$, and
  4598→\[
  4599→\RetKLc(M)
  4600→=
  4601→\min_{\substack{\phi\ \text{unifilar-lumpable}\\ |\mathcal K|\le M}}
  4602→\RetKLc(\phi).
  4603→\]
  4604→Blocks of zero stationary mass are ignored.
  4605→\end{definition}
  4606→
  4607→The independence of the input from the state is a genuine hypothesis and not a
  4608→normalization: it is what makes $\mathbb P(S_t=s\mid K_t=k,X_t=x)$ equal to
  4609→$\pi_s/\pi(C_k)$, and hence what identifies the block centroid with a
  4610→conditional law.  Remark~\ref{rem:controlled-ib-independence} records what
  4611→fails without it.
  4612→
  4613→\begin{theorem}[Controlled Information-Bottleneck Identity]
  4614→\label{thm:controlled-ib}
  4615→Under the hypotheses of Definition~\ref{def:controlled-full-kl}, for every
  4616→unifilar-lumpable $\phi$,
  4617→\[
  4618→\RetKLc(\phi)
  4619→=
  4620→I(S;Y\mid K_\phi,X)
  4621→=
  4622→I(S;Y\mid X)-I(K_\phi;Y\mid X),
  4623→\]
  4624→and consequently
  4625→\[
  4626→\RetKLc(M)
  4627→=
  4628→I(S;Y\mid X)
  4629→-
  4630→\max_{\substack{\phi\ \text{unifilar-lumpable}\\|\mathcal K|\le M}}
  4631→I(K_\phi;Y\mid X).
  4632→\]
  4633→The optimal representative is the controlled mixture centroid
  4634→\[
  4635→\bar P_k^x
  4636→=
  4637→\frac{\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_sP_s^x}{\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s}.
  4638→\]
  4639→All the mutual informations are finite, since $S$ is finite and
  4640→$I(S;Y\mid X)\le H(S)$.
  4641→\end{theorem}
  4642→
  4643→\begin{proof}
  4644→Fix an input $x$.  Conditionally on $X_t=x$, the block cost
  4645→$\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_sD_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_s^x\|Q_k^x)$ is minimized over $Q_k^x$
  4646→at the mixture centroid $\bar P_k^x$, by Lemma~\ref{lem:mixture-centroid}
  4647→applied to the family $\{P_s^x:s\in C_k\}$ with weights proportional to
  4648→$\pi_s$.  Substituting the centroids and summing against $p(x)$,
  4649→\[
  4650→\RetKLc(\phi)
  4651→=
  4652→\mathbb E\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}
  4653→\bigl(P_{Y\mid S,X}\,\big\|\,P_{Y\mid K,X}\bigr),
  4654→\]
  4655→where the identification $P_{Y\mid K,X}(\cdot\mid k,x)=\bar P_k^x$ uses
  4656→independence of $X_t$ from $S_t$: it gives
  4657→$\mathbb P(S_t=s\mid K_t=k,X_t=x)=\pi_s/\pi(C_k)$ for $s\in C_k$, so the
  4658→conditional law of $Y_t$ given $(K_t,X_t)=(k,x)$ is precisely the
  4659→$\pi$-weighted mixture $\bar P_k^x$.  Since $K=\phi(S)$ is a function of $S$,
  4660→we have $P_{Y\mid S,K,X}=P_{Y\mid S,X}$, and the displayed expectation is by
  4661→definition $I(S;Y\mid K,X)$.
  4662→
  4663→The chain rule for conditional mutual information gives
  4664→\[
  4665→I(S;Y\mid X)=I(K;Y\mid X)+I(S;Y\mid K,X),
  4666→\]
  4667→valid because $K$ is a function of $S$, whence the subtracted form.  The
  4668→rearrangement is legitimate because $I(S;Y\mid X)\le H(S)<\infty$, so no
  4669→difference of infinities arises.  Minimizing over unifilar-lumpable quotients
  4670→with at most $M$ blocks turns the identity into the stated variational form.
  4671→\end{proof}
  4672→
  4673→\begin{remark}[Role of the Independence Hypothesis]
  4674→\label{rem:controlled-ib-independence}
  4675→Definition~\ref{def:unifilar-machine} requires only that the input process be
  4676→stationary and ergodic, which permits $X_t$ to be correlated with $S_t$.  Under
  4677→such correlation the two sides of Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib} differ: the
  4678→conditional weight of $s$ within its block given $X_t=x$ is not
  4679→$\pi_s/\pi(C_k)$, so the centroid $\bar P_k^x$ is not a conditional law.
  4680→Already with two states, binary inputs and a ternary output alphabet, a
  4681→non-product stationary law of $(S,X)$ makes the quantity of
  4682→Definition~\ref{def:controlled-full-kl} exceed $I(S;Y\mid X)-I(K;Y\mid X)$ by
  4683→more than $0.13$ nats on the one-block quotient.
  4684→
  4685→The hypothesis is a property of the cost functional rather than of the
  4686→machine, and it can be removed by measuring the cost against the fiberwise
  4687→conditional weights.  This is carried out in
  4688→Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib-general} below, of which
  4689→Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib} is the independent case.
  4690→\end{remark}
  4691→
  4692→\begin{definition}[Fiberwise Controlled Retention Gap]
  4693→\label{def:controlled-full-kl-general}
  4694→Let $(S_t,X_t)$ have an arbitrary stationary joint law, with input marginal
  4695→$p(x)=\mathbb P(X_t=x)$ and fiberwise conditional state weights
  4696→\[
  4697→\pi_s^x=\mathbb P(S_t=s\mid X_t=x),
  4698→\qquad
  4699→x\in\supp p .
  4700→\]
  4701→For a unifilar-lumpable quotient $\phi$ with blocks $C_k$ and representatives
  4702→$\{Q_k^x\}$, define
  4703→\[
  4704→\RetKLg(\phi)
  4705→=
  4706→\sum_xp(x)\sum_k\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s^x\,
  4707→D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(P_s^x\,\big\|\,Q_k^x\bigr),
  4708→\]
  4709→the representatives being optimized for each $\phi$, and
  4710→\[
  4711→\RetKLg(M)
  4712→=
  4713→\min_{\substack{\phi\ \text{unifilar-lumpable}\\|\mathcal K|\le M}}
  4714→\RetKLg(\phi).
  4715→\]
  4716→Fibers and blocks of zero mass are ignored.  When $X_t$ is independent of
  4717→$S_t$ one has $\pi_s^x=\pi_s$ and
  4718→Definition~\ref{def:controlled-full-kl} is recovered.
  4719→\end{definition}
  4720→
  4721→\begin{theorem}[Controlled Information-Bottleneck Identity, General Inputs]
  4722→\label{thm:controlled-ib-general}
  4723→Let $(S_t,X_t)$ be stationary with arbitrary dependence, and let $\phi$ be
  4724→unifilar-lumpable.  Then
  4725→\[
  4726→\RetKLg(\phi)
  4727→=
  4728→I(S;Y\mid K_\phi,X)
  4729→=
  4730→I(S;Y\mid X)-I(K_\phi;Y\mid X),
  4731→\]
  4732→and consequently
  4733→\[
  4734→\RetKLg(M)
  4735→=
  4736→I(S;Y\mid X)
  4737→-
  4738→\max_{\substack{\phi\ \text{unifilar-lumpable}\\|\mathcal K|\le M}}
  4739→I(K_\phi;Y\mid X).
  4740→\]
  4741→The optimal representative is the fiberwise conditional centroid
  4742→\[
  4743→\bar P_k^x
  4744→=
  4745→\frac{\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s^xP_s^x}{\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s^x}
  4746→=
  4747→\mathcal L\bigl(Y_t\mid K_t=k,\ X_t=x\bigr).
  4748→\]
  4749→\end{theorem}
  4750→
  4751→\begin{proof}
  4752→Fix $x\in\supp p$ and a block $C_k$ of positive conditional mass.  Conditionally
  4753→on $X_t=x$, the inner sum $\sum_{s\in C_k}\pi_s^xD_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_s^x\|Q_k^x)$
  4754→is minimized over $Q_k^x$ at the $\pi^x$-weighted mixture of the $P_s^x$, by
  4755→Lemma~\ref{lem:mixture-centroid} applied with weights proportional to
  4756→$\pi_s^x$.  That mixture is exactly
  4757→$\mathcal L(Y_t\mid K_t=k,X_t=x)$: conditioning on $X_t=x$ first and on the
  4758→block second,
  4759→\[
  4760→\mathbb P(S_t=s\mid K_t=k,X_t=x)
  4761→=
  4762→\frac{\pi_s^x}{\sum_{s'\in C_k}\pi_{s'}^x}
  4763→\qquad(s\in C_k),
  4764→\]
  4765→which holds for \emph{any} joint law of $(S_t,X_t)$, no independence being
  4766→used; independence was needed in Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib} only to
  4767→replace $\pi_s^x$ by $\pi_s$.  Substituting the centroids,
  4768→\[
  4769→\RetKLg(\phi)
  4770→=
  4771→\mathbb E\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}
  4772→\bigl(P_{Y\mid S,X}\,\big\|\,P_{Y\mid K,X}\bigr)
  4773→=
  4774→I(S;Y\mid K,X),
  4775→\]
  4776→the last equality being the definition of conditional mutual information,
  4777→together with $P_{Y\mid S,K,X}=P_{Y\mid S,X}$ because $K=\phi(S)$.  The chain
  4778→rule $I(S;Y\mid X)=I(K;Y\mid X)+I(S;Y\mid K,X)$ gives the subtracted form, and
  4779→is legitimate since $I(S;Y\mid X)\le H(S)<\infty$.  Minimizing over
  4780→unifilar-lumpable quotients with at most $M$ blocks yields the variational
  4781→statement.
  4782→\end{proof}
  4783→
  4784→\begin{corollary}[Elementary Properties, General Inputs]
  4785→\label{cor:controlled-elementary-general}
  4786→$0\le\RetKLg(M)\le I(S;Y\mid X)$ with $\RetKLg(1)=I(S;Y\mid X)$; the gap is
  4787→nonincreasing in $M$; and $\RetKLg(\phi)\le\RetKLg(\psi)$ whenever the
  4788→unifilar-lumpable $\phi$ refines $\psi$.
  4789→\end{corollary}
  4790→
  4791→\begin{proof}
  4792→As in Corollary~\ref{cor:controlled-elementary}, every step being conditional
  4793→on $X$ and using Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib-general} in place of
  4794→Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib}.
  4795→\end{proof}
  4796→
  4797→\begin{corollary}[Fiberwise Spectral Converse, General Inputs]
  4798→\label{cor:controlled-simplex-general}
  4799→For finite $\mathcal O$, let $\Sigma_p^{x}$ denote the covariance of the
  4800→predictive probability vectors $\{p_s^x\}$ under the \emph{conditional} weights
  4801→$\{\pi_s^x\}$.  Then
  4802→\[
  4803→\RetKLg(M)
  4804→\ \ge\
  4805→\sum_xp(x)\sum_{i\ge M}\lambda_i\bigl(\Sigma_p^{x}\bigr).
  4806→\]
  4807→\end{corollary}
  4808→
  4809→\begin{proof}
  4810→On each fiber the family $\{p_s^x\}$ carries the probability weights
  4811→$\{\pi_s^x\}$ and the quotient is the same for every fiber, the lumpability
  4812→constraint being $x$-independent.  Theorem~\ref{thm:global-kl-simplex} applies
  4813→verbatim on the fiber with those weights; multiplying by $p(x)$ and summing
  4814→gives the bound.
  4815→\end{proof}
  4816→
  4817→\begin{remark}[The Reweighting Is Not Cosmetic]
  4818→\label{rem:controlled-general-reweighting}
  4819→In Corollary~\ref{cor:controlled-simplex-general} the fiber covariance must be
  4820→formed with the conditional weights $\pi_s^x$.  Substituting the unconditional
  4821→weights $\pi_s$, which is the form that Corollary~\ref{cor:controlled-simplex-spectral}
  4822→inherits from the independent case, produces a quantity that is not a lower
  4823→bound: among randomly generated instances with correlated $(S,X)$ and all
  4824→budgets $M$, the unconditional-weight expression exceeds $\RetKLg(M)$ in
  4825→several per cent of cases, while the conditional-weight bound of
  4826→Corollary~\ref{cor:controlled-simplex-general} is never violated.  Dependence
  4827→between input and state therefore changes the operative covariance, not only
  4828→the block weights.
  4829→\end{remark}

  4831→\begin{corollary}[Range, One-State Value, and Refinement Monotonicity, Controlled]
  4832→\label{cor:controlled-elementary}
  4833→$0\le\RetKLc(M)\le I(S;Y\mid X)$, with
  4834→$\RetKLc(1)=I(S;Y\mid X)$; if $\phi$ refines $\psi$ and both are
  4835→unifilar-lumpable then
  4836→$\RetKLc(\phi)\le\RetKLc(\psi)$; and
  4837→$\RetKLc(M)$ is nonincreasing in $M$.
  4838→\end{corollary}
  4839→
  4840→\begin{proof}
  4841→This is Corollary~\ref{cor:retention-elementary} with every quantity
  4842→conditioned on $X$.  A single block makes $K$ constant, so $I(K;Y\mid X)=0$;
  4843→refinement is data processing conditional on $X$, since $K_\psi$ is then a
  4844→function of $K_\phi$; and the feasible sets are nested in $M$.  Feasibility of
  4845→the one-block quotient is Definition~\ref{def:unifilar-lumpable}.
  4846→\end{proof}
  4847→
  4848→The zero-retention threshold does \emph{not} transfer verbatim.  In the
  4849→input-driven theory the singleton quotient is lumpable and the predictive
  4850→partition can always be realized, so the threshold is the number of distinct
  4851→predictive laws (Theorem~\ref{thm:retention-zero}).  For unifilar machines the
  4852→partition by predictive kernel need not be unifilar-lumpable, and the correct
  4853→threshold is the index of its coarsest unifilar-lumpable refinement.
  4854→
  4855→Corollary~\ref{cor:controlled-elementary} is the controlled counterpart of the
  4856→elementary facts recorded in Corollary~\ref{cor:retention-elementary}, and the
  4857→boundary behaviour of the natural-parameter converses is unchanged under
  4858→control, as Remark~\ref{rem:controlled-fisher-nogo} records.

  4860→\begin{definition}[Kernel Partition and Its Stable Refinement]
  4861→\label{def:kernel-refinement}
  4862→Let $\phi_{\ker}$ be the partition of $\Splus$ by predictive kernel, so
  4863→$\phi_{\ker}(s)=\phi_{\ker}(s')$ if and only if $P_s^x=P_{s'}^x$ for every $x$
  4864→in the input support.  Define $\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}$ to be the coarsest
  4865→unifilar-lumpable quotient refining $\phi_{\ker}$, and write
  4866→$N^{\ast}=|\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}(\Splus)|$ for its index.
  4867→\end{definition}
  4868→
  4869→\begin{proposition}[Existence of the Stable Refinement]
  4870→\label{prop:kernel-refinement-exists}
  4871→$\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}$ exists and is unique, and is computed by the refinement
  4872→recursion
  4873→\[
  4874→\phi^{(0)}=\phi_{\ker},
  4875→\qquad
  4876→\phi^{(m+1)}(s)
  4877→=
  4878→\Bigl(\phi^{(m)}(s),\ \bigl(\phi^{(m)}(\tau(s,x,y))\bigr)_{(x,y)\ \text{feasible from }s}\Bigr),
  4879→\]
  4880→which stabilizes after at most $|\Splus|$ steps.
  4881→\end{proposition}
  4882→
  4883→\begin{proof}
  4884→Call a partition \emph{admissible} if it is unifilar-lumpable and refines
  4885→$\phi_{\ker}$.  The singleton partition is admissible, so the family is
  4886→nonempty.  The recursion produces a decreasing chain of partitions, each
  4887→refining $\phi_{\ker}$, in a set of size $|\Splus|$; the number of blocks is
  4888→nondecreasing and bounded by $|\Splus|$, so the chain stabilizes after at most
  4889→$|\Splus|$ steps, at a partition $\phi^{\infty}$.  At the fixed point,
  4890→$\phi^{\infty}(s)=\phi^{\infty}(s')$ implies
  4891→$\phi^{\infty}(\tau(s,x,y))=\phi^{\infty}(\tau(s',x,y))$ for every commonly
  4892→feasible $(x,y)$, which is unifilar lumpability with
  4893→$\tau_{\mathcal K}(\phi^{\infty}(s),x,y)=\phi^{\infty}(\tau(s,x,y))$; and
  4894→$\phi^{\infty}$ refines $\phi_{\ker}=\phi^{(0)}$ by construction.  So
  4895→$\phi^{\infty}$ is admissible.
  4896→
  4897→For maximality, let $\psi$ be any admissible partition.  We show by induction
  4898→that $\psi$ refines $\phi^{(m)}$ for every $m$.  For $m=0$ this is the
  4899→hypothesis that $\psi$ refines $\phi_{\ker}$.  If $\psi$ refines $\phi^{(m)}$
  4900→and $\psi(s)=\psi(s')$, then $\phi^{(m)}(s)=\phi^{(m)}(s')$, and unifilar
  4901→lumpability of $\psi$ gives $\psi(\tau(s,x,y))=\psi(\tau(s',x,y))$ at every
  4902→commonly feasible event, hence
  4903→$\phi^{(m)}(\tau(s,x,y))=\phi^{(m)}(\tau(s',x,y))$ by the inductive
  4904→hypothesis; the two defining data of $\phi^{(m+1)}$ therefore agree, so
  4905→$\phi^{(m+1)}(s)=\phi^{(m+1)}(s')$.  Taking $m$ at the stabilization index
  4906→shows $\psi$ refines $\phi^{\infty}$.  Thus $\phi^{\infty}$ is the coarsest
  4907→admissible partition, and uniqueness follows.
  4908→\end{proof}
  4909→
  4910→\begin{theorem}[Zero Controlled Retention]
  4911→\label{thm:controlled-zero}
  4912→$\RetKLc(M)=0$ if and only if $M\ge N^{\ast}$, with $N^{\ast}$
  4913→the index of Definition~\ref{def:kernel-refinement}.
  4914→\end{theorem}
  4915→
  4916→\begin{proof}
  4917→Suppose $\RetKLc(\phi)=0$ for a unifilar-lumpable $\phi$.  The
  4918→cost is a sum of nonnegative terms $p(x)\pi_sD_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_s^x\|\bar P_k^x)$,
  4919→so every term vanishes; by the equality condition for KL divergence,
  4920→$P_s^x=\bar P_{\phi(s)}^x$ for every positive-mass $s$ and every $x$ in the
  4921→input support.  Two states in a common block therefore have equal kernels, so
  4922→$\phi$ refines $\phi_{\ker}$, and being unifilar-lumpable it is admissible in
  4923→the sense of Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists}.  Hence $\phi$
  4924→refines $\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}$ and has at least $N^{\ast}$ blocks.
  4925→
  4926→Conversely $\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}$ is itself unifilar-lumpable and refines
  4927→$\phi_{\ker}$, so each of its blocks carries a single kernel, every block
  4928→centroid equals that kernel, and the cost is zero.  It has $N^{\ast}$ blocks,
  4929→so $M\ge N^{\ast}$ suffices.
  4930→\end{proof}

  4932→\begin{remark}[The Threshold Is Not the Number of Distinct Kernels]
  4933→\label{rem:controlled-zero-not-kernels}
  4934→In the input-driven setting of Theorem~\ref{thm:retention-zero} the threshold
  4935→is the number of distinct predictive laws, because the predictive partition is
  4936→automatically lumpable there.  In the unifilar class the two quantities
  4937→separate, and the gap $N^{\ast}-|\phi_{\ker}(\Splus)|$ can be positive.  Take $\mathcal I$ a singleton, $\mathcal O=\{0,1\}$, three states
  4938→$A,B,C$, kernels
  4939→\[
  4940→P_A=P_B=\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\tfrac12),
  4941→\qquad
  4942→P_C=\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\tfrac1{10}),
  4943→\]
  4944→and the unifilar update
  4945→\[
  4946→\tau(A,0)=A,\quad\tau(A,1)=C,\qquad
  4947→\tau(B,0)=C,\quad\tau(B,1)=B,\qquad
  4948→\tau(C,0)=A,\quad\tau(C,1)=B .
  4949→\]
  4950→The stationary distribution is strictly positive.  There are two distinct
  4951→kernels, but the partition $\{\{A,B\},\{C\}\}$ is not unifilar-lumpable: $A$ and
  4952→$B$ are in one block and the output $0$ is feasible from both, yet
  4953→$\tau(A,0)=A$ and $\tau(B,0)=C$ lie in different blocks.  The refinement of
  4954→Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists} separates $A$ from $B$, so
  4955→$N^{\ast}=3$ while $|\phi_{\ker}(\Splus)|=2$, and indeed
  4956→$\RetKLc(2)>0$ whereas $\RetKLc(3)=0$.  The
  4957→separation is not exceptional: over randomly generated unifilar machines with
  4958→at most five states and at least one coincidence of predictive kernels, the
  4959→kernel partition fails to be unifilar-lumpable in approximately fifty-five per
  4960→cent of instances.
  4961→
  4962→This is exactly the distinction between predictive equivalence and causal-state
  4963→equivalence recorded in Definition~\ref{def:z-predictive-equivalence}: the
  4964→refinement $\phi_{\ker}^{\,\ast}$ is the causal-state partition for the full
  4965→controlled future, and $\phi_{\ker}$ is its one-step coarsening.
  4966→\end{remark}
  4967→
  4968→The separation of Remark~\ref{rem:controlled-zero-not-kernels} is as large as
  4969→the state set permits, and the refinement recursion is essentially as slow as
  4970→Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists} allows.  Both are witnessed by
  4971→one family.

  4973→\begin{definition}[Counter Family $\mathcal C_M$]
  4974→\label{def:counter-family}
  4975→For $M\ge3$ and $\beta,\gamma\in(0,1)$ with $\beta\neq\gamma$, let
  4976→$\mathcal C_M$ be the unifilar machine with a single input letter, output
  4977→alphabet $\mathcal O=\{0,1\}$, state set $\{0,1,\dots,M-1\}$, update
  4978→\[
  4979→\tau(s,0)=\min\{s+1,\,M-1\},
  4980→\qquad
  4981→\tau(s,1)=0,
  4982→\]
  4983→and emission laws
  4984→\[
  4985→P_s=\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\beta)\ \ (0\le s\le M-2),
  4986→\qquad
  4987→P_{M-1}=\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\gamma).
  4988→\]
  4989→Output $0$ advances the counter and saturates at $M-1$; output $1$ resets it.
  4990→The restriction to the open interval is required for
  4991→Proposition~\ref{prop:refinement-extremal}(i): at $\gamma=0$ the state $M-1$
  4992→emits $0$ almost surely and, being saturated, is absorbing, while at $\beta=1$
  4993→every state below $M-1$ resets almost surely and $M-1$ is never reached.  In
  4994→either case the chain fails to be irreducible and some state leaves the
  4995→stationary support.  The input alphabet being a singleton, $X_t$ is
  4996→deterministic and hence independent of $S_t$, so
  4997→Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-ib} applies and $\RetKLg$ coincides with
  4998→$\RetKLc$ on this family.
  4999→\end{definition}

  5001→\begin{proposition}[The Refinement Gap Is Maximal and the Bound Is Tight]
  5002→\label{prop:refinement-extremal}
  5003→Let $\beta,\gamma\in(0,1)$ with $\beta\neq\gamma$.  For $\mathcal C_M$:
  5004→\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  5005→\item the chain is irreducible with strictly positive stationary
  5006→distribution, so $\Splus$ is the whole state set;
  5007→\item the kernel partition has exactly two blocks,
  5008→$|\phi_{\ker}(\Splus)|=2$;
  5009→\item the stable refinement is the discrete partition, $N^{\ast}=M$, so the
  5010→gap $N^{\ast}-|\phi_{\ker}(\Splus)|=M-2$ is the largest possible on $M$
  5011→states;
  5012→\item the refinement recursion of
  5013→Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists} stabilizes after exactly
  5014→$M-1$ steps, so its bound of $|\Splus|$ steps is tight to within one;
  5015→\item consequently $\RetKLc(M-1)>0$ while $\RetKLc(M)=0$.
  5016→\end{enumerate}
  5017→\end{proposition}
  5018→
  5019→\begin{proof}
  5020→\emph{(i)} Every state emits both outputs with positive probability: states
  5021→$0,\dots,M-2$ because $\beta\in(0,1)$, and state $M-1$ because
  5022→$\gamma\in(0,1)$.  Consequently output $1$ carries any state to $0$, and a run
  5023→of $M-1$ outputs $0$ carries $0$ to $M-1$, so every state reaches every other.
  5024→The chain is therefore irreducible on a finite set, and its stationary
  5025→distribution is strictly positive, so $\Splus=\{0,\dots,M-1\}$.
  5026→
  5027→\emph{(ii)} States $0,\dots,M-2$ carry the law $\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\beta)$ and
  5028→state $M-1$ carries $\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\gamma)\neq\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\beta)$,
  5029→giving exactly two kernel classes.
  5030→
  5031→\emph{(iii)} and \emph{(iv)} Write $\phi^{(m)}$ for the $m$-th iterate, with
  5032→$\phi^{(0)}=\phi_{\ker}$. We claim, by induction on $m\ge0$, that $\phi^{(m)}$
  5033→separates exactly the top $m+1$ states, that is
  5034→\[
  5035→\phi^{(m)}(s)=\phi^{(m)}(s')
  5036→\iff
  5037→s=s'
  5038→\ \text{ or }\
  5039→s,s'\le M-2-m .
  5040→\]
  5041→For $m=0$ this is~(ii). Assume it for $m$ and let $s<s'\le M-2-m$. The
  5042→recursion refines by the pair of successor classes
  5043→$\bigl(\phi^{(m)}(\tau(s,0)),\phi^{(m)}(\tau(s,1))\bigr)$. Since
  5044→$s,s'\le M-2-m\le M-2$, neither is saturated, so $\tau(s,0)=s+1$ and
  5045→$\tau(s',0)=s'+1$, while $\tau(s,1)=\tau(s',1)=0$. By the inductive hypothesis
  5046→$s+1$ and $s'+1$ are separated precisely when at least one of them exceeds
  5047→$M-2-m$, that is precisely when $s'=M-2-m$. Hence $\phi^{(m+1)}$ splits
  5048→$M-2-m$ off from the states below it and leaves $0,\dots,M-3-m$ together,
  5049→which is the claim for $m+1$.
  5050→
  5051→At $m=M-2$ the block $\{s:s\le M-2-m\}=\{0\}$ is a singleton, so
  5052→$\phi^{(M-2)}$ is the discrete partition and $N^{\ast}=M$, giving~(iii). The
  5053→recursion detects stabilization one step later, at $m=M-1$, since
  5054→$\phi^{(M-2)}$ and $\phi^{(M-1)}$ have the same number of blocks while
  5055→$\phi^{(M-3)}$ has fewer; the number of iterations is therefore exactly $M-1$,
  5056→which is~(iv). The bound $N^{\ast}\le|\Splus|$ of
  5057→Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists} is attained, and no partition
  5058→of an $M$-element set has more than $M$ blocks, so the gap $M-2$ is maximal.
  5059→
  5060→\emph{(v)} Immediate from Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-zero} and $N^{\ast}=M$.
  5061→\end{proof}

  5063→\begin{remark}[Scope of the Extremal Family]
  5064→\label{rem:refinement-extremal-scope}
  5065→Proposition~\ref{prop:refinement-extremal} settles the worst case, not the
  5066→typical one. It shows that no bound of the form
  5067→$N^{\ast}\le f(|\phi_{\ker}(\Splus)|)$ can hold, since $\mathcal C_M$ has two
  5068→kernel classes for every $M$, and that the round count of
  5069→Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel-refinement-exists} cannot be improved below
  5070→$|\Splus|-1$. It uses a single input letter, so the phenomenon is driven
  5071→entirely by the output-dependence of the update and is invisible to any
  5072→input-driven analysis. For $M=3,\dots,7$ the values $\RetKLc(M-1)$ are
  5073→$0.0481$, $0.0321$, $0.0192$, $0.0107$ and $0.0057$ nats at
  5074→$\beta=\tfrac12$, $\gamma=\tfrac1{10}$, decreasing in $M$ as the extra
  5075→resolved state carries less stationary mass.
  5076→\end{remark}

  5078→\begin{corollary}[Recovery of the Input-Driven Theory]
  5079→\label{cor:controlled-reduces}
  5080→Let the emissions be output-independent, $P_s^x=P_s$ for every $x$, as in every
  5081→construction of Section~\ref{subsec:retention-complexity}.  Then
  5082→$\RetKLc(\phi)=\RetKL(\phi)$ for every quotient $\phi$ feasible
  5083→in both theories.  If moreover the machine is input-driven and every quotient
  5084→under consideration has connected support --- in particular if every $P_s$ has
  5085→full support --- the two feasible sets coincide by
  5086→Proposition~\ref{prop:input-driven-specialization}(i), and therefore
  5087→$\RetKLc(M)=\RetKL(M)$ for every $M$, the controlled identity
  5088→reducing to Meta-Theorem~\ref{meta:ib}.

  5091→\begin{proof}

  5094→the two minima requires equality of the feasible sets over which they are
