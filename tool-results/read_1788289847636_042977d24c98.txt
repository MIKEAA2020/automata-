 17101→\item Tagged depth-two NP-hardness in the same regime.
 17102→\item Commitment exact threshold
 17103→\[
 17104→\Com(M)=0
 17105→\iff
 17106→M\ge\kappa_{\det}(F).
 17107→\]
 17108→\item Safety controller complexity $\kappa_{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\Lambda)$.
 17109→\item Strategic-spread lower bound with explicit myopic adversary.
 17110→\item Stateless-game exact corollary $\ComGame(M)=\alpha/(1-\gamma)$.
 17111→\item Booleanized symmetrized $\alpha=0$ commitment vertex.
 17112→\item Unrestricted linear Hankel relaxation converse
 17113→\[
 17114→\Dunres(M)
 17115→=
 17116→\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu).
 17117→\]
 17118→\item Hankel-structured lower bound
 17119→\[
 17120→\DHankstr(M)\ge\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu).
 17121→\]
 17122→
 17123→\item Stochasticity floor for deterministic Mealy grounding, and its exact
 17124→observable refinement: $\sigma_\gamma(\nu)\le F_\gamma(\nu)$ with
 17125→$F_\gamma(\nu)$ the exact input-only deterministic floor and
 17126→$\lim_{M\to\infty}\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)=F_\gamma(\nu)$
 17127→(Theorem~\ref{thm:observable-floor}).
 17128→\item Finite-section operator certificates for linear grounding: computable
 17129→two-sided bounds on $\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu)=\Dunres(M)$ from finite Hankel
 17130→truncations $H_N$, with an explicit lower certificate
 17131→$\DHankstr(M)\ge\sigma_{M+1}(H_N)$ for the structured problem
 17132→(Proposition~\ref{prop:grounding-finite-section}).
 17133→\item Exact zero threshold for the Hankel-structured linear problem:
 17134→$\DHankstr(M)=0\iff\rank(H)\le M$
 17135→(Proposition~\ref{prop:grounding-structured-zero}).
 17136→\item Finite-horizon symbolic certificates: a two-sided sandwich
 17137→$\Delta^{(T)}_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)\le\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)\le
 17138→\Delta^{(T)}_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)+\gamma^T/(1-\gamma)$, decidable and
 17139→rational under rational finite-state and rational-$\gamma$ hypotheses
 17140→(Proposition~\ref{prop:symbolic-finite-horizon}).
 17141→\item Refinement monotonicity of the tracking deficit: $\phi$ refining $\psi$
 17142→implies $D(\phi)\le D(\psi)$, so $\min_{|\mathcal K|\le M}D(\phi)$ is
 17143→nonincreasing in $M$ (Correction to item (iii) of
 17144→Proposition~\ref{prop:grounding-tracking}).
 17145→\item Joint zero-error memory theorem via meet of right congruences.
 17146→\item Comparative state-complexity sandwich.
 17147→\item Independence of regime obstructions.
 17148→\item Interaction theorem for exact commitment and approximate retention.
 17149→\item Price of Safety in mutual-information form.
 17150→\item Reset-word realizable mistake complexity $\Theta(M\log M)$.
 17151→\item \textbf{Persistent-stream realizable mistake complexity
 17152→$\Theta(M\log M)$}, with the lower bound proved by an explicit transport-plus-
 17153→readout adversary on a single never-reset stream
 17154→(Theorem~\ref{thm:stream-lower-bound}).
 17155→\item Active upper bound $O(\Esync(M))$ under objective~(RI)
 17156→(Theorem~\ref{thm:active-certified}(I)), and
 17157→$O(M\log M+\Lsyncu(M))$ in universal length form
 17158→(Proposition~\ref{prop:active-length-upper}).
 17159→\item \textbf{Active realizable mistake complexity
 17160→$\Theta(M\log M)$, unconditionally}: an active halving learner attains
 17161→objective~(RI) within $\log_2|\mathcal H_M\times Q|=O(M\log M)$ mistakes
 17162→(Theorem~\ref{thm:active-halving}), matching the gated-family lower bound, so
 17163→$\Esync(M)=\Theta(M\log M)$ with no synchronizability,
 17164→operational-equivalence, or direct-sum hypothesis
 17165→(Corollary~\ref{cor:active-theta}).
 17166→\item \textbf{Explicit gated family} $\Gact_M$ with
 17167→$\Esync(M)=\Omega(M\log M)$, giving an active lower bound
 17168→$\Omega(M\log M)$ relative to the residual-identification objective
 17169→(Theorem~\ref{thm:active-explicit-directsum}).  The unknown datum is a
 17170→transition map, so the bound is for model identification; it does not witness a
 17171→state-identification component.
 17172→\item Conditional additive active lower bound under direct-sum saturation
 17173→(Theorem~\ref{thm:active-direct-sum}), which separates the two terms; the
 17174→unconditional active lower bound itself is
 17175→Corollary~\ref{cor:active-theta}.
 17176→\item \textbf{Agnostic minimax regret $\Theta(\sqrt{T M\log M})$} in both the
 17177→reset-word and persistent-stream protocols
 17178→(Theorem~\ref{thm:agnostic}, Corollary~\ref{cor:stream-ldim}).
 17179→\item Fixed-budget bias--variance decomposition.
 17180→\item Adaptive agnostic oracle inequality under bounded losses.
 17181→\item Realizable $0/1$ and mixable log-loss oracle inequalities under stated
 17182→conditions.
 17183→\item Linear pinching Price-of-Safety surrogate nonnegativity and Ky Fan
 17184→majorization.
 17185→\item Retention as exact $\alpha=1$ vertex.
 17186→\item Commitment as Booleanized symmetrized $\alpha=0$ vertex.
 17187→\item Grounding as $\alpha=\infty$ supremum/operator-norm face, related but
 17188→not identical to max-divergence.
 17189→\end{itemize}

 17200→assumptions.

 17212→\item Linear block-local Price-of-Safety surrogate.  It is unconditionally

 17215→discrepancy $\rho_{\mathrm{safe}}-\rho_{\mathrm{free}}$, neither term of which
 17216→has a determined sign (Section~\ref{subsec:pos-discrete}).

 17218→requiring a regime-specific response operator and domination bridge rather
 17219→than following from aggregation
 17220→(Heuristic~\ref{heur:spectral}).

 17222→between R\'enyi order and Schatten exponent and carries no derivation of the
 17223→Mirsky converses (Section~\ref{sec:exponent}).

 17225→threshold rather than an ordinary Schatten $p\to0$ limit
 17226→(Theorem~\ref{thm:commitment-alpha0}).

 17228→value only under a Hardy-space embedding
 17229→(Theorems~\ref{thm:aak-equality} and~\ref{thm:aak-multiletter}).
 17230→\item The block-mode tracking construction, which realizes a deterministic
 17231→Mealy machine of the claimed stationary error only under the explicit
 17232→input-driven synchronization and right-congruence implementability
 17233→hypothesis of Proposition~\ref{prop:block-mode-implementability}; absent that
 17234→hypothesis the same error formula is only the loss of a genie that observes
 17235→the latent block label.

 17238→%----------------------------------------------------------------------
 17239→\subsection{Open Problems}
 17240→\label{sec:openproblems}
 17241→\label{subsec:open-problems}
 17242→%----------------------------------------------------------------------
 17243→
 17244→The following open problems remain.
 17245→
 17246→\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em]
 17247→\item \textbf{Exact symbolic deterministic grounding gap (partially resolved).}
 17248→\emph{Hypotheses.} $0<\gamma<1$, finite input alphabet, target channel $\nu$.
 17249→\emph{Established baseline.} Theorem~\ref{thm:observable-floor} identifies
 17250→the exact input-only deterministic floor $F_\gamma(\nu)$ and proves
 17251→$\lim_{M\to\infty}\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)=F_\gamma(\nu)$, with
 17252→$\sigma_\gamma(\nu)\le F_\gamma(\nu)$ possibly strict.
 17253→Proposition~\ref{prop:symbolic-finite-horizon} supplies, for every finite
 17254→horizon $T$, a two-sided certified interval for
 17255→$\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}^{\mathrm{symb}}(M)=\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}(M;\gamma)$ of
 17256→width at most $\gamma^T/(1-\gamma)$, decidable and rational for rational
 17257→finite-state channels and rational $\gamma$.  What remains open is a
 17258→\emph{closed-form value at finite $M$}, not merely a limit or a certified
 17259→interval, and its relation to the unrestricted linear finite-rank Hankel gap
 17260→$\Dunres(M)=\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu)$, to the stochasticity floor $\sigma_\gamma$,
 17261→and to the superpolynomial determinism gap of
 17262→Theorem~\ref{thm:exp-gap} (see Open Problem~\ref{open:symbolic-grounding}).
 17263→\emph{Success criterion.} A formula or algorithm computing
 17264→$\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}^{\mathrm{symb}}(M)$ exactly for every finite $M$ (not
 17265→just $M\to\infty$ or a fixed horizon $T$), together with either a proof or a
 17266→counterexample to $\Delta_{\mathrm{grd}}^{\mathrm{symb}}(M)\ge\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu)$
 17267→for all $M$.
 17268→
 17269→\item \textbf{Hankel-structured equality conditions.}
 17270→Characterize necessary and sufficient conditions under which
 17271→\[
 17272→\DHankstr(M)
 17273→=
 17274→\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu).
 17275→\]
 17276→Theorem~\ref{thm:aak-equality} settles the sufficient direction: a Hardy-space
 17277→symbol with $\varphi\in H^\infty+C(\mathbb T)$ forces equality for
 17278→\emph{every} $M$, with an attained optimum of rational symbol degree at most
 17279→$M$.  Corollary~\ref{cor:hankel-strict} converts this into a necessary
 17280→condition on the operator: a strict gap at any $M$ rules out Hardy-space
 17281→representability.  What remains open is the intrinsic characterization -- an
 17282→$\ell^2((\mathcal I\times\mathcal O)^*)$-side criterion, stated directly in
 17283→terms of $\nu$, deciding when such a symbol exists.
 17284→
 17285→\item \textbf{Relaxation gaps for the Price of Safety.}
 17286→Prove explicit bounds relating the linear block-local Price-of-Safety
 17287→surrogate
 17288→\[
 17289→\PoSlin(M)
 17290→\]
 17291→to the discrete right-congruence Price of Safety
 17292→\[
 17293→\PoSquad(M)
 17294→\]
 17295→or the full-KL Price of Safety.
 17296→
 17297→\item \textbf{Exact and fixed-alphabet complexity of full-KL retention.}
 17298→The unrestricted full-KL decision problem is: given $\{P_s\}$, $\{\pi_s\}$, a
 17299→budget $M\in\mathbb N$, lumpability constraints, and $\tau$, decide whether
 17300→\[
 17301→\min_{\substack{\phi\ \text{lumpable}\\|\mathcal K|\le M}}
 17302→\sum_k\sum_{s:\phi(s)=k}\pi_s\KL\bigl(P_s\|\bar P_k\bigr)
 17303→\le\tau .
 17304→\]
 17305→The budget $M$ is present because it is what makes the problem a genuine
 17306→finite-state compression decision rather than a bare clustering feasibility
 17307→question at the natural number of distinct rows; the case $M\ge|\Splus|$ is
 17308→trivial by Theorem~\ref{thm:retention-zero}, so the decision problem is of
 17309→interest exactly for $M<|\Splus|$.
 17310→Because the optimal block representative is the mixture centroid
 17311→$\bar P_k$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:mixture-centroid}), the objective is exactly the
 17312→weighted \emph{Jensen--Shannon} clustering cost, so the problem is
 17313→\emph{constrained Jensen--Shannon clustering with lumpability constraints}.
 17314→The Gaussian quadratic regime of Theorem~\ref{thm:retention-reset-np} is its
 17315→second-order Fisher special case, where the centroid degenerates to a mean and
 17316→the cost to squared Euclidean distance, which is how the $k$-means reduction
 17317→applies there.  That reduction is carried out in Theorem~\ref{thm:full-kl-promise-np}: the
 17318→obstruction --- that KL centroids are not rational in the inputs, so the
 17319→promise gap cannot be produced by denominator clearing --- is met by embedding
 17320→$k$-means into a shrinking interior neighbourhood of the uniform law, where the
 17321→Jensen--Shannon cost is a controlled perturbation of the Euclidean one and the
 17322→gap is preserved analytically.  Four questions remain.
 17323→
 17324→\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2.0em]
 17325→\item \textbf{Membership in NP.}  The objective compares a rational threshold
 17326→against a sum of terms $p\log(p/q)$ with $p,q$ rational.  Deciding such
 17327→inequalities in polynomial time is not known, so exact NP-completeness is open
 17328→even though hardness is proved.
 17329→
 17330→Proposition~\ref{prop:full-kl-algebraic-form} locates the difficulty exactly.
 17331→For a fixed partition the whole sum collapses,
 17332→$\RetKL(\phi)=D^{-1}\log R$ with $D$ a positive integer and $R$ a positive
 17333→rational presented as a product of powers, so the comparison with $\tau$ is
 17334→equivalent to deciding $R\ge e^{D\tau}$.  The number of logarithmic terms is
 17335→therefore not the obstruction; the single rational-versus-exponential
 17336→comparison is.  That comparison is decidable, by
 17337→Lindemann--Weierstrass as recorded in
 17338→Remark~\ref{rem:full-kl-exp-membership}, and what is missing is a polynomial
 17339→bound on the precision needed to resolve it, equivalently a polynomial lower
 17340→bound on $|R-e^{D\tau}|$.  Remark~\ref{rem:full-kl-slp} shows that $R$ must be
 17341→kept in factored form for this to be a meaningful question, its expanded
 17342→numerator and denominator being of exponential bit size.  Consequently the
 17343→problem lies in NP as soon as this $\textsc{PosSLP}$-type comparison admits
 17344→polynomial-time verifiable certificates.
 17345→\item \textbf{Fixed output alphabet.}  The embedding of
 17346→Theorem~\ref{thm:full-kl-promise-np} uses $|\mathcal O|=2d$, growing with the
 17347→dimension of the $k$-means instance.  Whether the problem is hard for a fixed
 17348→alphabet, binary in particular, is open.
 17349→
 17350→Remark~\ref{rem:output-alphabet-2d} determines how far the alphabet can be
 17351→reduced within this scheme, and the answer is arithmetic rather than a
 17352→universal constant: $|\mathcal O|$ may be taken as small as the least
 17353→$n\ge d+1$ whose zero-sum subspace in $\mathbb Q^{n}$ contains $d$ pairwise
 17354→orthogonal rational vectors of equal norm.  The value $n=2d$ always qualifies,
 17355→by the doubling map; $n=d+1$ qualifies for $d=3,7,15,\dots$, by the
 17356→non-constant rows of a Hadamard matrix of order $d+1$; and $n=d+1$ fails for
 17357→$d=2$, by a discriminant obstruction.  What no choice of scheme can avoid is
 17358→$|\mathcal O|\ge d+1$, since the zero-sum subspace of
 17359→$\mathbb R^{|\mathcal O|}$ has dimension $|\mathcal O|-1$ and must accommodate
 17360→$d$ independent directions.  A fixed alphabet therefore caps the embeddable
 17361→dimension, and a fixed-alphabet hardness proof would have to start from a
 17362→source problem hard in bounded dimension rather than from Euclidean
 17363→$k$-means.
 17364→\item \textbf{Hardness without a promise.}  The reduction separates instances
 17365→whose values differ by at least the amplified gap; exact comparison against a
 17366→rational threshold is a different question.
 17367→\item \textbf{Nonvacuous lumpability.}  The reduction makes every partition
 17368→lumpable.  The complexity under genuinely restrictive right-congruence
 17369→constraints is not addressed.
 17370→\end{enumerate}
 17371→
 17372→\item \textbf{Domination bridges.}
 17373→Identify task-theoretic conditions under which contextwise aggregation
 17374→implies Schatten domination, or under which frame-type lower bounds hold.
 17375→
 17376→\item \textbf{Intermediate exponent regimes.}
 17377→Determine whether intermediate R\'enyi orders
 17378→\[
 17379→\alpha\in(0,\infty)\setminus\{1\}
 17380→\]
 17381→can be made operational as finite-state approximation regimes with matching
 17382→rank-control and domination structures.
 17383→
 17384→\item \textbf{Active learning without synchronization.}
 17385→Develop active realizable and agnostic bounds when no output-aware
 17386→synchronizing word exists, or when synchronization is only partial.
 17387→
 17388→\item \textbf{Hardy-space embeddings for grounding.}
 17389→Identify when a Hankel operator admits a Hardy-space embedding making
 17390→Adamjan--Arov--Krein applicable, and compare the resulting converses with
 17391→the default Eckart--Young--Mirsky converse.
 17392→
 17393→\item \textbf{Deterministic commitment rate-distortion.}
 17394→\emph{Hypotheses.} A fixed history law $\mu$ on $\mathcal I^{\mathbb N}$ and a
 17395→causal length-preserving specification $F$ (Definition~\ref{def:causal-lp-spec}).
 17396→\emph{Established baseline.} Theorem~\ref{thm:com-rd-formula} gives the exact
 17397→fixed-budget value of $\ComRD(M)$ over right-congruence quotients of a
 17398→one-step observable, and its zero threshold at
 17399→$M=\kappa_{\mathrm{obs}}(F^{(1)},\mu)$; Remark~\ref{rem:com-rd-scope} shows
 17400→this is generically distinct from, and no more than, the worst-case Boolean
 17401→gap $\Comex(M)$.  What is open is the \emph{full multi-step} rate-distortion
 17402→law: the exact value of the $M$-state quotient loss for approximating the
 17403→entire residual process $(F_u)_{u\in\mathcal I^*}$ under $\mu$-average loss,
 17404→not just its one-step marginal, together with a converse comparable in
 17405→strength to the Blahut--Arimoto-type characterization available for classical
 17406→rate-distortion.
 17407→\emph{Success criterion.} A formula (or a provably tight two-sided bound with
 17408→matching rate) for the $M$-state $\mu$-average multi-step commitment
 17409→distortion, together with a decidable finite-horizon certificate analogous to
 17410→Proposition~\ref{prop:symbolic-finite-horizon}.
 17411→
 17412→\item \textbf{Typed joint quotients across the linear/symbolic boundary.}
 17413→\emph{Hypotheses.} Two tasks, one governed by a right-congruence quotient
 17414→budget (commitment, retention, or symbolic grounding under
 17415→Proposition~\ref{prop:block-mode-implementability}) and one governed by the
 17416→unrestricted linear finite-rank Hankel relaxation of
 17417→Theorem~\ref{thm:spectral-grounding}.
 17418→\emph{Established baseline.} Theorem~\ref{thm:comparative} and
 17419→Remark~\ref{rem:comparative-grounding-excluded} prove the product sandwich
 17420→\emph{only} when every component's budget is a right-congruence index, and
 17421→explicitly exclude the unrestricted linear coordinate: no representative
 17422→map is known that combines an $M$-state quotient with a rank-$M'$ Hankel
 17423→truncation into one object of controlled joint budget.
 17424→What is open is whether \emph{any} nontrivial joint budget notion
 17425→$M_{\mathrm{joint}}$, mixing a quotient index and an operator rank, admits
 17426→even a one-sided analogue of the sandwich of Theorem~\ref{thm:comparative}.
 17427→\emph{Success criterion.} Either (a) a bridge construction -- a map from
 17428→rank-$M'$ Hankel approximants to $O(f(M'))$-state quotients (or conversely)
 17429→that preserves admissibility and control loss by a stated factor, from which
 17430→a mixed sandwich would follow by composing with Theorem~\ref{thm:comparative};
 17431→or (b) a proof, e.g.\ via an explicit family of channels, that no such bridge
 17432→with subexponential $f$ can exist.
 17433→
 17434→\item \textbf{Strategic memory versus information.}
 17435→\emph{Hypotheses.} A commitment task admitting both the distributional gap
 17436→$\ComRD(M)$ and the simultaneous-move strategic gap $\ComGame(M)$ of
 17437→Section~\ref{subsec:quant-simul}.
 17438→\emph{Established baseline.} Remark~\ref{rem:com-rd-scope} proves these two
 17439→quantities are posed on different protocols (averaging under a fixed law
 17440→versus play against an online adversary) and asserts no general inequality
 17441→between them; Section~\ref{subsec:quant-simul} computes $\ComGame(M)$ exactly
 17442→in the stateless-game case.  What is open is whether, under an explicit
 17443→correspondence between the adversary's input distribution and a history law
 17444→$\mu$ (e.g.\ the adversary's minimax-optimal mixed strategy), $\ComGame(M)$
 17445→and $\ComRD(M)$ become comparable -- whether strategic memory (states needed
 17446→to play well against an adaptive adversary) is provably bounded above or
 17447→below by informational memory (states needed to match a fixed one-step
 17448→predictive law).
 17449→\emph{Success criterion.} Either a proved inequality
 17450→$\ComGame(M)\le g(\ComRD(M'))$ or $\ComRD(M)\le g(\ComGame(M'))$ for an
 17451→explicit function $g$ and budget map $M\mapsto M'$, under a stated
 17452→correspondence between the adversary's strategy and $\mu$; or an explicit
 17453→task family showing the two quantities are order-independent (each can be
 17454→made arbitrarily larger than the other at matching budgets), closing the
 17455→question negatively.
 17456→
 17457→\item \textbf{Necessity of the aggregation penalty for nested classes.}
 17458→Proposition~\ref{prop:aggregation-necessary} settles necessity for arbitrary
 17459→experts; what remains is the nested case.
 17460→Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle-minimax-lower} proves a two-sided minimax
 17461→characterization of the adaptive oracle envelope under the explicit
 17462→approximation and estimation floors of
 17463→Assumption~\ref{ass:oracle-floors}, matching the adaptive upper bound up to
 17464→the aggregation penalty $\PsiAgg_M(T)$ and universal constants.  The floors are
 17465→discharged for the realizable persistent-stream regime by
 17466→Proposition~\ref{prop:floors-instance} and assumed in the stochastic regimes.
 17467→What remains open is whether $\PsiAgg_M(T)$ is itself necessary, i.e.\ whether
 17468→adapting to an unknown budget is strictly harder than the oracle problem, and
 17469→whether horizon-efficient packings exist for the retention, grounding, and
 17470→commitment regimes.  See Remark~\ref{rem:oracle-minimax-residual}.
 17471→\end{enumerate}

 17482→%======================================================================
 17483→\section{Conclusion}
 17484→\label{sec:conclusion}
 17485→%======================================================================
 17486→
 17487→Finite-index right congruences on histories provide a common combinatorial
 17488→resource for the three regimes, while the analytical engines governing them
 17489→remain regime-specific.  The principal conclusions are the following.
 17490→
 17491→\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em]
 17492→\item \textbf{Shared syntax, regime-specific semantics.}
 17493→Regime-typed histories, finite-index right-action quotients, and variational
 17494→gaps form a common finite-state syntax: input histories for commitment,
 17495→feasible joint histories for unifilar retention, the joint alphabet for
 17496→grounding.  Commitment, retention, and grounding attach different semantic
 17497→objects to states: residual functions, predictive distributions or kernels,
 17498→and operators or kernels.
 17499→
 17500→\item \textbf{Stationary aggregation.}
 17501→The average-case theory uses stationary Ces\`aro aggregation or discounted
 17502→prefix measures.  Finite prefixes do not define a probability measure over
 17503→all lengths.
 17504→
 17505→\item \textbf{Retention has two distinct gaps.}
 17506→The full-KL gap satisfies an exact finite-state information-bottleneck
 17507→decomposition over stationary lumpable quotients:
 17508→\[
 17509→\RetKL(M)
 17510→=
 17511→I(S;Z)
 17512→-
 17513→\max_{\substack{
 17514→\phi\ \text{lumpable}\\
 17515→|\mathcal K|\le M
 17516→}}
 17517→I(K_\phi;Z).
 17518→\]
 17519→Zero retention occurs exactly when $M$ is at least the stationary support size
 17520→of distinct predictive states.  The Gaussian quadratic restricted gap satisfies
 17521→a spectral lower bound with effective rank $M-1$.  Full KL has a precise local
 17522→Fisher asymptotic lower bound under regularity assumptions.
 17523→
 17524→The same analysis holds for general unifilar controlled machines: the
 17525→controlled information-bottleneck decomposition conditional on the input, and
 17526→fiberwise quadratic, probability-coordinate, and interior-Fisher spectral
 17527→converses, with the input-driven theory as the output-independent
 17528→specialization.  The zero-retention threshold, by contrast, does not transfer
 17529→verbatim: it is the index of the coarsest unifilar-lumpable refinement of the
 17530→predictive-kernel partition, which can strictly exceed the number of distinct
 17531→kernels (Theorem~\ref{thm:controlled-zero}).
 17532→
 17533→\item \textbf{Grounding spectral converses are linear finite-rank.}
 17534→The unrestricted linear finite-rank relaxation satisfies
 17535→\[
 17536→\Dunres(M)
 17537→=
 17538→\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu)
 17539→\]
 17540→by Eckart--Young--Mirsky.  The Hankel-structured finite-rank realization gap
 17541→satisfies
 17542→\[
 17543→\DHankstr(M)
 17544→\ge
 17545→\sigma_{M+1}(H_\nu),
 17546→\]
 17547→with equality only under additional structural hypotheses, and finite
 17548→Hankel truncations supply computable two-sided certificates for both
 17549→quantities (Proposition~\ref{prop:grounding-finite-section}).  The structured
 17550→problem has an exact zero threshold at $\rank(H)\le M$
 17551→(Proposition~\ref{prop:grounding-structured-zero}).  For the symbolic
 17552→deterministic (Mealy-machine) grounding gap, the exact input-only
 17553→deterministic floor $F_\gamma(\nu)$ and its finite-state limit are now
 17554→theorems (Theorem~\ref{thm:observable-floor}), with decidable finite-horizon
 17555→certified intervals (Proposition~\ref{prop:symbolic-finite-horizon}); a
 17556→closed-form value at finite $M$, and its relation to the linear relaxation,
 17557→remain open (Open Problem~\ref{open:symbolic-grounding}).  AAK is used only
 17558→under separate Hardy-space embeddings.
 17559→
 17560→\item \textbf{Commitment is Boolean rank.}
 17561→Exact commitment is governed by Myhill--Nerode index.  The exponent
 17562→correspondence identifies commitment with the Booleanized symmetrized
 17563→$\alpha=0$ vertex, not with raw $D_0$ and not with ordinary Schatten rank.
 17564→
 17565→\item \textbf{The spectral-tail picture is a heuristic.}
 17566→Spectral tails arise from regime-specific response operators, effective rank
 17567→budgets, and domination bridges.  Aggregation alone does not determine the
 17568→tail.  No unconditional cross-regime ordering follows.
 17569→
 17570→\item \textbf{The Schatten template is conditional.}
 17571→Mirsky's theorem unifies the analytic converse form once a Schatten
 17572→domination bridge is supplied.  Grounding is the $p=\infty$ instance for the
 17573→unrestricted linear finite-rank relaxation; retention is the $p=1$ instance
 17574→with effective rank $M-1$; commitment is the Boolean-rank vertex.
 17575→
 17576→\item \textbf{The oracle bridge is adaptive and type-correct.}
 17577→Offline approximation and online estimation compose into a bias--variance
 17578→decomposition.  Adaptive aggregation attains a penalized minimum without
 17579→knowledge of the optimal budget.  Spectral tails govern online rates only
 17580→under explicit achievability assumptions.
 17581→
 17582→\item \textbf{The oracle floors are discharged in one regime only.}
 17583→Assumption~\ref{ass:oracle-floors} is a minimax separation hypothesis.  It is
 17584→established unconditionally for the realizable persistent-stream regime by
 17585→Proposition~\ref{prop:floors-instance}, where the packing is the gated family
 17586→$\Gact_M$ and $\Delta_M=M\log M$; there
 17587→Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle-minimax-lower} is a theorem rather than a reduction.
 17588→For the retention, grounding, and commitment regimes the floors are not
 17589→established, and there the theorem remains a conditional reduction.  The
 17590→discharged case is realizable, so it certifies the estimation axis only; by
 17591→Remark~\ref{rem:floors-two-axes} the sum-form envelope follows from the minimum
 17592→form through the discrete sandwich, and by Lemma~\ref{lem:kappa-bounded} the
 17593→resulting constant $(1+\kappa)^{-1}$ is absolute rather than
 17594→instance-dependent.  See Section~\ref{sec:openproblems}.
 17595→
 17596→\item \textbf{The matching minimax lower bound is conditional on explicit
 17597→floors.}
 17598→An adaptive oracle upper bound and a conditional continuous bias--variance
 17599→sandwich hold in general, and under the floors of
 17600→Assumption~\ref{ass:oracle-floors} a matching minimax lower bound holds as well
 17601→(Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle-minimax-lower}); the envelope is therefore optimal up
 17602→to the aggregation penalty $\PsiAgg_M(T)$.  The constant in the sum-form
 17603→version is absolute, since Lemma~\ref{lem:kappa-bounded} bounds the jump ratio
 17604→by $2^\alpha(1+\log2)^\beta$ for a regularly varying estimation envelope.  The
 17605→floors themselves are proved for the realizable persistent-stream regime
 17606→(Proposition~\ref{prop:floors-instance}) and assumed elsewhere.  Whether the
 17607→penalty $\PsiAgg_M(T)$ is necessary, and whether the floors hold in the
 17608→stochastic regimes at horizons $T$ of the order at which the envelopes cross,
 17609→remain open.
 17610→
 17611→\item \textbf{The linear Price-of-Safety surrogate is exact but distinct.}
 17612→Pinching gives an exact majorization law for a linear second-order,
 17613→state-indexed, block-local surrogate.  It does not compute the discrete
 17614→right-congruence Price of Safety without relaxation-error control.
 17615→
 17616→\item \textbf{The exponent correspondence is a vertex correspondence.}
 17617→R\'enyi order, Schatten exponent, and aggregation label share vertex labels
 17618→$\{0,1,\infty\}$, but they are distinct layers.  The equality $\alpha=p$ is
 17619→a formal consistency, not a derivation.
 17620→
 17621→\item \textbf{Temporal claims are protocol-specific.}
 17622→For the reset-word protocol, realizable finite-automaton learning has
 17623→$\Theta(M\log M)$ mistake complexity.  The same $\Theta(M\log M)$ holds for a
 17624→single \emph{persistent} Mealy stream that is never reset: the upper bound is
 17625→finite-class counting, and the matching lower bound is proved in
 17626→Theorem~\ref{thm:stream-lower-bound} by an adversary that makes state
 17627→navigation free and extracts $\Omega(M\log M)$ bits through forced-mistake
 17628→readouts.  Finite-class aggregation gives agnostic regret
 17629→\[
 17630→O\bigl(\sqrt{T M\log M}\bigr).
 17631→\]
 17632→For active learning, all bounds are stated against the residual-identification
 17633→objective, an identification requirement being unavoidable in a protocol where
 17634→the learner selects its own inputs.  The sharp upper bound is
 17635→\[
 17636→\MistRI(M)
 17637→\le
 17638→O(\Esync(M)),
 17639→\]
 17640→with the universal length form $O(M\log M+\Lsyncu(M))$ when the learner must
 17641→fix its synchronization experiment in advance.  On the full class
 17642→$\mathcal H_M$ the complexity is settled outright,
 17643→\[
 17644→\MistRI(M)
 17645→=
 17646→\Theta(M\log M),
 17647→\]
 17648→the upper bound by an active halving learner over the version space of
 17649→machine-state pairs, the lower bound by the gated adversarial family.  The
 17650→$\Omega(M\log M)$ lower bound comes from that explicit construction,
 17651→not from ordinary Littlestone dimension, which does not transfer to a protocol
 17652→in which the learner chooses its own inputs.
 17653→
 17654→A direct-sum instance forces its two components on disjoint rounds, giving
 17655→$\MistRI(M)\ge S_M+C_M$, but no non-degenerate two-term decomposition of the
 17656→active complexity is available in the mistake currency: state identification in
 17657→a known minimal skeleton costs only $O_{|\mathcal O|}(\log M)$ mistakes
 17658→(Proposition~\ref{prop:esyncsi-log}).  Whether a genuine decomposition exists
 17659→in the experiment-length currency is
 17660→Open Problem~\ref{open:si-hard-family}.
 17661→
 17662→\item \textbf{NP-hardness is regime-qualified.}
 17663→The Gaussian quadratic restricted retention decision problem is NP-complete
 17664→already for minimal reset machines of synchronization depth one, and a tagged
 17665→depth-two construction also gives NP-hardness.  For unrestricted full-KL
 17666→retention over a finite output alphabet,
 17667→Theorem~\ref{thm:full-kl-promise-np} gives NP-hardness under a promise and
 17668→Corollary~\ref{cor:full-kl-apx} upgrades this to APX-hardness, so no
 17669→polynomial-time approximation scheme exists unless $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$.
 17670→Two qualifications attach to those two results, and both have been located
 17671→precisely.  The output alphabet produced by the embedding has size $2d$, so
 17672→hardness for a \emph{fixed} alphabet is not established; by
 17673→Remark~\ref{rem:output-alphabet-2d} the size can be reduced to $d+1$ exactly
 17674→when the zero-sum subspace of $\mathbb Q^{d+1}$ admits an orthogonal rational
 17675→basis of equal norms, which holds for $d=3,7,15,\dots$ and fails for $d=2$, but
 17676→it can never be taken below $d+1$, so the alphabet grows with the dimension
 17677→under any variant of the construction.  Membership in NP is open; by
 17678→Proposition~\ref{prop:full-kl-algebraic-form} the objective is $D^{-1}\log R$
 17679→for a factored rational $R$, so the question is the single comparison
 17680→$R\ge e^{D\tau}$, decidable by Lindemann--Weierstrass but lacking a polynomial
 17681→precision bound.
 17682→\end{itemize}
 17683→
 17684→Each result above is stated under the hypotheses given in the corresponding
 17685→section, and its type signature in the sense of
 17686→Definition~\ref{def:type-signature} fixes the regime, feasible set,
 17687→aggregation, support, protocol, and analytical bridge to which it applies.
